Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary depending on scope and complexity. A standard website typically takes 6–10 weeks; larger platform builds or strategy engagements may take 3–6 months. We'll give you a clear timeline during our initial consultation.

What information do you need to get started?

Just a brief overview of your goals and challenges is enough to start. We'll guide you through a discovery process to gather everything we need — you don't need a fully formed brief to reach out.

Do you work with clients outside the US?

Absolutely. We work with clients across 18 countries and are experienced in managing remote collaborations across time zones. We use async-friendly tools and regular video check-ins to keep things running smoothly.

What does your pricing look like?

We offer both fixed-price project engagements and retainer-based arrangements. Pricing depends on scope, complexity, and timeline. Contact us for a tailored proposal — we're transparent about costs from day one.
